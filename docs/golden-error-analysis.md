## Pattern conclusions

Generic praise/criticism: explicit other/complaint/severity-2 rules exist (33, 35, 39, 44), but vague “diverse” is borderline. Ads: explicit usability (27), including specifically praised ad behavior (35). Catalog/lyrics/recommendations: explicit catalog (30), subject to multi-issue precedence (35). Paid-plan mention: explicit non-billing exception (36), while actual paywalls belong to billing (31). Mixed issues: explicit severity-first/tie-first rule (35), but impact itself can be ambiguous. Non-English: declared label-normally-if-understood rule, translation uncertainty retained. Political/unrelated: explicit bare-slogan exception (39); do not ignore genuine product claims. Pure requests: explicit severity 1 (43); this is the clearest golden-side pipeline problem above. Historical resolution: temporal precedence is not explicitly specified. Restricted versus blocked function: exact definitions at 45–46; neither assumption of remaining streaming nor assumption of complete blockage is licensed by missing context (49).

No prompt, threshold, routing decision or golden label was changed by this audit. The separate development evidence and recommendation are in `docs/offline-preproduction-gate.md`.
